# Lane 3.5 Kinetic effectors, branch `calib/p3-kinetic`, model tag ids `interceptor-miss` and `gun-burst`

This lane fits a statistical estimate to a published datasheet value. Validation means the model reproduces the vendor number.

Files owned: `src/playback/pk/kinetic.ts`, `src/playback/ballistics.ts` (new), `src/playback/intercept.ts`, their tests, `kinetic.verify.test.ts`, your rows in `src/analysis/fidelity.ts` and `fidelity.test.ts`, and the fine-step hook line in `src/playback/attrition.ts` (`commitSalvo`, marked with a hook comment: the one call `clock.requestFineStep(...)` for a projectile group; nothing else in that file).

Deliverable (plan row 3.5). Interceptor: a speed profile from `boostTimeS` to `maxFlyoutSpeedMps` then `flyoutSpeedMps` as the cruise (a null profile field keeps the constant `flyoutSpeedMps` flyout); miss distance sigma = root sum square of the track prediction error at launch (`missDistanceSigmaM` already roots the estimate covariance) and `guidanceNoiseM`; P(effect) (probability of effect) = P(miss < `lethalRadiusM`) for a Rayleigh miss with that sigma (state the closed form), times the existing multi-effect and evasion terms only where they still apply (say which you keep and why); `seekerAcquisitionRangeM` gates the terminal phase: a launch whose predicted intercept point puts the seeker acquisition after the flyout end is infeasible. Projectile effector: projectiles per burst `burstRounds` at `rateOfFireRpm`, each projectile a Rayleigh miss with sigma = range times root sum square of `dispersionMils` and `fireControlErrorMils` (mils as milliradians) plus the track error; time of flight from `muzzleVelocityMps` with a simple drag-free or one-constant drag ballistic in `ballistics.ts` (state which and the source); P(effect) per burst = 1 - product over projectiles of (1 - P(int)), where P(int) (probability of intercept) = P(miss < target cross-section radius from `spanMFor`). The burst is one event: one draw keyed at the commit step, and one `clock.requestFineStep(effectorId, t, t + burstS, 0.1)` so the burst timeline samples at 0.1 s.

Fallback: the `legacyKineticPk` switch, or an effector with null `lethalRadiusM` (interceptor) or null `dispersionMils` (projectile effector), keeps today's `kineticPk` formula bit for bit; jammers, optical effector and RF field effector are lane 3.6's (`legacyBeamPk`) and stay on `kineticPk` in this lane.

Stop condition: a P(effect) table from an advertised effective radius is reproduced (hand-derive P(effect) at three miss sigmas for the Coyote or APKWS effective radius in the seed and pin them); the 0.1 s step test matches a hand-derived burst timeline (a 20-projectile burst at 1200 projectiles per minute is 1.0 s, projectiles at 0.05 s spacing, the effect time of the last projectile is commit plus burst plus time of flight, computed in the comment). Validation tier: `datasheet`.
